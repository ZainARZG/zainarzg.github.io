A CGPA looks clean when written on paper. Mine is 3.90588, and after first semester it placed me second in my class. I am proud of that. At the same time, I know how misleading a neat number can be when it hides the story behind it.

That story includes poor sleep, printing pressure, long nights with PF and AICT work, weak moments in theory-based subjects, and the emotional weight of trying to perform well away from home. It also includes the Honahaar Scholarship, which was a blessing, but even that came with paperwork, travel to Lahore, and submission complications that required patience and effort of their own.

I also know which subjects helped reveal the best side of me. PF, Calculus I, Discrete Mathematics, AICT, and the labs matched the way I think. Fehm ul Quran and parts of Applied Physics reminded me where discipline and adjustment were still needed. Both types of subjects were important.

What matters most to me now is not only the result itself, but the habits that built it. Those habits are what I need to carry into OOP, DBS, Statistics, and Calculus II if I want my next semester to be strong for the right reasons.
