--- layout: post title: "The Day Lahore Took My Energy and Faisalabad Tested My Patience" date: 2025-03-06 00:00:00 +0500 summary: "One repair trip showed me how much physical and mental effort can hide behind something as simple as keeping a student laptop alive." subpoints: - "I spent the day in Lahore for laptop repair while trying to manage routes I did not know well." - "The bus back to Faisalabad was sold out but still took us there, and after that I still walked about 4 kilometres to my hostel." - "All day I had only a packet of grains, and when I finally reached my room I ate first and only then opened my laptop to make sure it was still okay for my PF and lab work." ---

I had one day in first semester that I do not think I will forget for a long time. My laptop needed repair, and in Computer Engineering that is never a small issue. The laptop is not just a device. It is where the labs, assignments, code, and routine all meet. So I went to Lahore to get it fixed.

Lahore itself exhausted me that day because I was unfamiliar with the routes and had to move around more than expected. The whole day, the only thing I ate was a packet of grains. By the time the repair work was done, I already felt physically drained.

The return journey still had another layer of difficulty. The bus to Faisalabad was sold out, but it still ran and brought us back. After reaching Faisalabad, I still had to walk about four kilometres to the hostel because the university area is not highly urban and getting transport there is not always easy.

When I finally reached my room, I ate a proper meal first. Only after that did I open the laptop to check that everything was still fine. I did not use it for a lab that night. I just needed to know that the tool I depend on so much was still standing.
