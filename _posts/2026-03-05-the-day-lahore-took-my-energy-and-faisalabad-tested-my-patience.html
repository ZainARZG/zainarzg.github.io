Why the trip mattered so much

In Computer Engineering, a laptop is not just another device. It is where assignments, code, labs, and project work all come together. So when mine needed repair, the issue immediately became serious. I went to Lahore because I needed to get it handled properly, even though moving around there was never very comfortable for me.

The city itself drained me that day because I was unfamiliar with the routes and had to keep figuring things out while already tired. By the time the repair work was finished, the whole day had passed and I had eaten almost nothing except a packet of grains.

Why the return journey stayed in my memory

The bus back to Faisalabad was sold out, but it still ran and reached there. Even after that, the day was not really over for me. Once I arrived in Faisalabad, I still had to walk around four kilometres to get back to the hostel because the university area is not highly urban and transport there is not always easy to find.

I remember that walk because it did not feel dramatic in a cinematic way. It felt quiet, heavy, and real. It was the kind of student struggle that does not usually appear in official records, but shapes the way you experience a semester from the inside.

What I did when I finally reached the room

When I got back to the hostel, I ate properly first. Only after that did I open the laptop, not to do a lab, but simply to check that everything was still okay. That small moment meant a lot to me. It was not about productivity. It was about relief.
