--- layout: post title: "Why Database Systems Did Not Begin With SQL for Me - It Began With Rejection" date: 2025-03-04 00:00:00 +0500 summary: "My second semester DBS journey became meaningful the moment my first project idea was rejected." subpoints: - "In second semester, Database Systems introduced me to SQL, structure, and a more organized way of thinking." - "My WordPress, XAMPP, WAMP, and e-commerce dropshipping idea was rejected because it did not match local production." - "That rejection from Dr Bilal pushed me toward AI, ML, and DL thinking and ended up fitting my real interests better than my first idea." ---

Database Systems entered my second semester at the right time because I was ready for another logic-based subject. SQL immediately gave me a different kind of satisfaction. Instead of thinking only about program flow like in PF, I now had to think about data, structure, relationships, and how information should be organized correctly.

My first project idea, however, did not survive. Sir initially asked for a website project using WordPress, XAMPP or WAMP, and MySQL. I suggested a dropshipping e-commerce model that would source products from places like Daraz and Amazon. Dr Bilal rejected it because he wanted students to work with ideas tied more directly to things available or produced in local contexts.

At that moment I had two options: feel blocked, or change direction honestly. When he suggested blog writing or AI, ML, and DL work, I chose the second path. That choice immediately felt more personal to me than the first idea had.

So for me, DBS did not begin as a list of SQL commands. It began as a lesson that rejection is sometimes just redirection toward something more accurate.
