What daily life actually looked like

When people look at results or course lists, they usually do not see the texture of day-to-day student life. In my first semester, that texture was intense. The hostel routine, assignment pressure, PF and AICT labs, report writing, printing issues, and submission timing were all constantly active in the background.

PF and AICT were central to that feeling because they demanded both thinking and execution. I often stayed up late trying to make my work cleaner and worth submitting. Sometimes I also helped groupmates with their lab reports, which made the load more real.

The part of AICT I remember with pride

One of my stronger memories from that semester is the AICT presentation under Munnazza. I chose Seerat ul Nabi (PBUH) as my topic, built the presentation carefully in PowerPoint, presented it seriously, and earned full marks. That mattered because it showed that I can communicate well when I prepare deeply enough.

It also reminded me that technical study should not make a person one-dimensional. I liked that this task asked for care, structure, and delivery all at once.

Why I count this as real responsibility

First semester made me more responsible because it removed the illusion that academic life is only about classes. I learned how much unseen effort holds the visible result together. That lesson has stayed with me, and it is one reason I now look at all my work with more seriousness.
