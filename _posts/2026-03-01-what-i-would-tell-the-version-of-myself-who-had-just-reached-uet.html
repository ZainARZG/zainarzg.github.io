I would only help him recognize what they are shaping." subpoints: - "I would tell my first-semester self to trust Programming Fundamentals because it will be the first subject that makes CE feel personal." - "I would tell my second-semester self to respect Database Systems because SQL is teaching me structure, not just queries." - "I would also say that hostel life, train rides home with friends and razai, and online classes after regional tension are all part of the same journey." ---

If I could talk to the version of myself who first arrived at UET Lahore Campus Faisalabad, I would not tell him that everything will become easy. I would tell him that the difficulty is real, but it is not random. It is preparing him for a clearer version of himself.

I would tell him to trust Programming Fundamentals early, because PF will become the first place where he stops feeling like a student who only likes technology and starts feeling like someone who can actually build with it. I would also tell him not to ignore the discipline part of student life, because staying up late feels powerful until morning consequences arrive.

I would tell my current self something too: respect Database Systems more deeply. SQL is not just another skill to list. It is training me to think in a more organized way. I need that mindset if I want to move seriously toward AI, ML, and bigger work later.

And finally, I would remind myself that the full journey includes more than classrooms. It includes hostel life, two close friends from Bhalwal, Abdullah Khalid and Abdullah Sikandar, a first train ride home with blankets in Ramadan, online classes during regional tension, and the quiet decision to keep going anyway. That is also engineering education, just in a different form.
