01 — My Story

Not the path I
was handed —
the one I chose.

I grew up in Bhalwal, Sargodha. Not as developed as Lahore, not as loud. It doesn't have everything — but it's mine and I love it. I loved computers before I knew what engineering was. When CE became an option, it was never a question.

First semester hit different from what I expected. All-nighters before mids and finals. PF and AICT labs built from scratch. A full day navigating an unfamiliar Lahore to repair my laptop — nothing to eat but a packet of grains the whole day, the bus back sold out but still running, then 4km on foot through Faisalabad, a proper meal when I finally reached my room. A Honahaar Scholarship trip with document complications I hadn't expected. A groupmate I got close to transferred to Lahore. I lost focus for a while. Then I studied harder than I ever had before.

I finished with a CGPA of 3.91 — 2nd highest in my class. Second semester is online now. Wars, petrol prices, Eid. Studying from home. But I'm still here, and I'm not stopping.

03 — Featured Project

Born from a
random conversation.

Machine Learning · Supervised Learning · Weather Forecasting · 1st Semester

Supervised Learning Approach for Next Day Temperature and Precipitation Forecasting in Basel

The idea didn't come from an assignment brief. I was talking to ChatGPT one night about some model or code — weather came up casually, and something clicked. When Dr. Bilal later talked about training a model in class, I remembered that conversation immediately. My collaborator Muhammad Tahir Yaqoob was also my roommate, which made the whole process seamless.

A supervised ML model that forecasts next-day temperature and precipitation using 10 years of historical weather data from 18 major European cities. Built on the European Cities Weather Prediction Dataset from Kaggle. Covers data preprocessing, feature engineering, model training and evaluation — applied to a real-world forecasting challenge. Presented at the 1st semester project exhibition in front of teachers, fellow students, and seniors.
