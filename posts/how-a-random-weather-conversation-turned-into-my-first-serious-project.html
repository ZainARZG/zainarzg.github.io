How a Random Weather Conversation Turned Into My First Serious Project

My first project that felt truly mine started from a small idea that stayed with me until it became buildable.

The idea did not feel academic when it first appeared. It felt casual. That is exactly why I remember the moment it became real.

Where the idea came from

What makes this project personal to me is that it did not begin inside a formal project template. I remember a conversation with ChatGPT that touched on weather and model-related thinking. Later, when Dr Bilal discussed training a model, that earlier thought returned and clicked into place with much more force.

That became the base of our first semester project: a supervised learning approach for next-day temperature and precipitation forecasting in Basel. We worked on it using a weather dataset that covered eighteen European cities over ten years. It was the first time I felt AI and Machine Learning move from curiosity into actual work.

Why the partnership worked

I built the project with Muhammad Tahir Yaqoob, and one reason the collaboration felt smooth was that he was also my roommate. That made communication easier and more natural. The work could continue even after class because we were already sharing the same environment.

That mattered a lot for my first serious project. It meant I could focus more on understanding the model, the data, and the structure of the work instead of wasting energy on avoidable coordination problems.

What the exhibition changed

Presenting the project in the exhibition in front of teachers, students, and seniors changed the way I saw it. Once you have to explain your work publicly, you either understand it or you discover the gaps. For me, that experience strengthened the project and made it feel like work I could stand behind.

Reflection Points

  • A weather-related conversation and Dr Bilal's class discussion pushed me toward my first ML project.
  • Working with Muhammad Tahir Yaqoob made the project feel smooth and serious.
  • The exhibition forced me to explain my work with confidence in front of others.
