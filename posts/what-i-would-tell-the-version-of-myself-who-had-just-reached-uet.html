Article 10PF and DBSLetter to Self

What I Would Tell the Version of Myself Who Had Just Reached UET

If I could speak to my earlier self, I would not remove the hard parts. I would explain them.

I would not promise ease. I would promise meaning, because that is what the difficult parts eventually turned into.

What I would say about Programming Fundamentals

I would tell my first-semester self to trust Programming Fundamentals very early. PF will become the first subject that feels deeply personal, not because it is easy, but because it matches the way you think. It will give you confidence before you fully understand how much you need that confidence.

I would also say not to be embarrassed by how much time you want to spend making code cleaner. That is not wasted time. It is one of the earliest signs of what kind of work ethic you are building.

What I would say about the harder parts

I would tell that earlier version of me that hostel life, tiredness, weak routines, emotional distraction, travel, and uncertainty are all real. They will affect focus. But they will not end the story unless you let them. You are going to keep moving even after days that feel heavier than they look from outside.

I would also tell myself that the people around me matter. The room matters. Home matters. The first train ride back with friends and razai, the two close friends in Bhalwal, the strange shift into online classes, all of it belongs to the same education.

What I would say now about Database Systems

I would tell my current self to take Database Systems seriously in a deeper way. SQL is not just another item to place in a skills list. It is training structure, order, and the habit of thinking clearly about information. That matters for where I want to go next.

If I had to reduce the whole message to one line, it would be this: stay with the work long enough for it to become yours. That is what PF taught me first, and it is what DBS is teaching me now in a different form.

Reflection Points

  • I would tell myself to trust Programming Fundamentals early because it would become personal.
  • I would also tell myself to respect Database Systems because SQL is teaching me structure.
  • Hostel life, travel, online classes, and home are all part of the same education.
