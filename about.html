About

A student story shaped by practical work, steady pressure, and long-term ambition.

I'm from Bhalwal, District Sargodha, and I study BS Computer Engineering at UET Lahore Campus Faisalabad. I chose this field because computers always held my attention early, but university taught me how much discipline, patience, and repetition it takes to turn interest into real ability.

Personal Direction

What keeps me moving forward.

  • I work best when a problem has logic, structure, and room for improvement.
  • I am naturally drawn toward software, data systems, and machine learning.
  • I want my academic foundation to grow into work that is original, disciplined, and technically strong.
01

How I approach learning

I connect most strongly with practical work, mathematics, and programming logic. I tend to stay with a problem until it makes sense, which is one reason I have always felt more at home in technical work that can be tested, improved, and built step by step.

02

What the first year taught me

My first semesters have been shaped by hostel life, labs, deadlines, travel, project presentations, and the kind of routine that demands more maturity than it first appears to. That pressure has also helped me understand how I work, where I perform well, and what kind of engineer I want to become.

03

Where I am headed

My next steps are about building more depth in OOP, SQL, and systems thinking while continuing to move toward AI and machine learning with better technical confidence and stronger project work.
