About Me

A student story shaped by discipline, late nights, and practical work.

I am from Bhalwal, District Sargodha, and I study BS Computer Engineering at UET Lahore Campus Faisalabad. I chose this field because computers and technology always attracted me, but university taught me that interest alone is not enough. I had to learn how to turn that interest into real work, real results, and real ownership.

01

Background and mindset

I am naturally curious and usually go beyond the syllabus. I am a night owl, a perfectionist, and someone who connects strongly with practical work, mathematics, and programming logic. People sometimes read my seriousness as attitude, but in reality it is focus.

02

First semester reality

First semester was heavier than I expected. I spent long nights on PF and AICT labs, handled lab reports and printing issues, travelled to Lahore for laptop repair and Honahaar scholarship documents, and still worked my way toward a strong academic result.

03

Second semester direction

In second semester, I am studying OOP in C#, Database Systems in SQL, Calculus II, Statistics, Digital Logic Design, and Communication Skills. SQL and data thinking are already strengthening my structure, while AI and ML keep pulling me toward my future direction.
