About Me

My name is Syed Muhammad Zain. I am from Bhalwal, District Sargodha, and I study BS Computer Engineering at UET Lahore Campus Faisalabad. I chose Computer Engineering because I have been drawn to computers and technology since childhood, and I wanted a field where logic, building, and curiosity could all live together.

I am the kind of student who naturally goes beyond the syllabus. I work best when I can understand something deeply instead of just memorizing it. That is why I connected early with Programming Fundamentals, practical labs, mathematics, and project work, while theory-heavy subjects felt more demanding for me.

Semester 1

My first semester was intense. I spent long nights on PF and AICT labs, managed printing and lab report problems, travelled to Lahore for both laptop repair and Honahaar scholarship paperwork, and still finished with a strong result. I also learned that discipline matters just as much as ability, especially when a night-owl routine starts colliding with morning classes.

Projects

Weather Prediction Model

My first major project was a machine learning model titled "Supervised Learning Approach for Next Day Temperature and Precipitation Forecasting in Basel." I built it with Muhammad Tahir Yaqoob, who was also my roommate, which made collaboration smooth. We worked with a ten-year dataset covering eighteen European cities and presented the project in the semester exhibition before teachers, students, and seniors.

Database Systems Direction Shift

In Database Systems, I first proposed a WordPress and MySQL based e-commerce idea using XAMPP and WAMP. It was rejected because the project needed stronger local relevance. That rejection mattered because it pushed me toward AI, ML, and DL thinking, and eventually toward pairing with Muhammad Umair when the project rules changed.
